\markboth{Problem 4}{Problem 4: Part 2}

\textbf{Problem Statement:} Consider the equation
$$f(x) = 0,$$
where
$$f(x) = x - \frac{1}{2}\left(\cos(x/2) - \left|x - \frac{1}{2}\right|\right).$$

Do the following.

\subsection*{Part 2}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Problem 4.2}

Write the computer program implementing the fixed point iteration $x_{n+1} = T(x_n)$. Discuss the choice of an initial guess $x_0$. Calculate the solution to within 10 decimal places. Note number of iterations needed.

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\vspace{1em}

\textbf{Solution:}

The fixed point iteration $x_{n+1} = T(x_n)$ was implemented in Julia. Any initial guess $x_0$ in $[0.45, 0.55]$ works due to contractivity; I chose $x_0 = 0.48$.

The method converged to $x \approx 0.4722515915$ in 23 iterations, accurate to 10 decimal places. The terminating condition was $|x_{n+1} - x_n| < 10^{-10}$, ensuring accuracy to 10 decimal places. The terminal output is shown in Figure~\ref{fig:p04-2-Tx-iterations}.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.45\textwidth]{figures/p04-2-Tx-iterations-terminal-output.png}
    \caption{Fixed point iteration output for $T(x)$, showing convergence.}
    \label{fig:p04-2-Tx-iterations}
\end{figure}

The result verifies existence and uniqueness of the solution in the interval as concluded in 4-1, as expected from the contraction mapping theorem.
