\subsection*{Part 1 (Textbook 4.2.1)}

Prove these facts, needed in the proof of Theorem 2.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item If $U$ is upper triangular and invertible, then $U^{-1}$ is upper triangular.
    \item The inverse of a unit lower triangular matrix is unit lower triangular.
    \item The product of two upper (lower) triangular matrices is upper (lower) triangular.
\end{enumerate}
