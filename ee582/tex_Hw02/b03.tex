	\textbf{Discussion:}

The figure above presents a direct comparison of the RL circuit response for all six cases:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{PSCAD:} Simulated with $h=0.1$ ms and $h=0.8$ ms (reference standard).
	\item \textbf{Trapezoidal:} MATLAB implementation with $h=0.1$ ms and $h=0.8$ ms.
	\item \textbf{Backward Euler:} MATLAB implementation with $h=0.1$ ms and $h=0.8$ ms.
\end{itemize}

	\textit{Accuracy:} For $h=0.1$ ms, all methods closely match, with Trapezoidal and PSCAD nearly indistinguishable. For $h=0.8$ ms, the Backward Euler method shows more numerical damping, while Trapezoidal and PSCAD remain close, though some time-step artifacts are visible.

	\textit{Time-step Impact:} Larger $h$ increases numerical error, especially for Backward Euler (overdamping). Trapezoidal is more accurate for both $i(t)$ and $v_L(t)$ at larger $h$.

	\textit{PSCAD vs Trapezoidal:} The MATLAB Trapezoidal method matches PSCAD very closely for both time steps, confirming the correctness of the implementation and the equivalence of the underlying algorithms.
