\subsubsection*{Trapezoidal Rule}

For an inductor with $v(t) = L \frac{di(t)}{dt}$, the trapezoidal discretization (derivation in \Cref{sec:appendix_ind_trapz}) yields:

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=burntOrange!5, colframe=burntOrange!80!black, title={\color{white}\textbf{Trapezoidal: Inductor Companion Model}}]
\begin{align}
    \Aboxed{v(t) &= R_L i(t) - e_h} \label{eq:vt_ind_trapz} \\
    \Aboxed{i(t) &= \frac{v(t)}{R_L} + i_h} \label{eq:it_ind_trapz}
\end{align}
where:
\begin{align}
    R_L &= \frac{2L}{h} \\
    e_h &= R_L i(t-h) + v(t-h) \\
    i_h &= \frac{e_h}{R_L} = i(t-h) + \frac{v(t-h)}{R_L}
\end{align}
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsubsection*{Backward Euler}

For the same inductor, the Backward Euler discretization (derivation in \Cref{sec:appendix_ind_be}) yields:

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=deepBlue!5, colframe=deepBlue!80!black, title={\color{white}\textbf{Backward Euler: Inductor Companion Model}}]
\begin{align}
    \Aboxed{v(t) &= R_L i(t) - e_h} \label{eq:vt_ind_be} \\
    \Aboxed{i(t) &= \frac{v(t)}{R_L} + i_h} \label{eq:it_ind_be}
\end{align}
where:
\begin{align}
    R_L &= \frac{L}{h} \\
    e_h &= R_L i(t-h) \\
    i_h &= i(t-h) = \frac{e_h}{R_L}
\end{align}
\end{tcolorbox}
